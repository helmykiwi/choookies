# Choookies Dough

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript for a lightweight localhost-only landing page.

## Users

Cookie lovers in Malaysia looking for a shareable box of handmade cookies, either as an easy treat or a gift.

## Product Purpose

Choookies Dough makes small crunchy-chewy cookies and large gooey-chewy cookies available in a choice of familiar chocolate-forward flavours.

## Positioning

The product is defined by its two cookie experiences: small, crunchy and still chewy; or large, gooey and chewy.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the cookie styles, flavours, package sizes and listed RM prices, then use the order call to action to begin an enquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a landing page only; no checkout, Firebase, GitHub or database integration.
- Product facts and package prices must come from the supplied price image.
- A contact channel has not been supplied, so the order action displays a clear local enquiry prompt rather than inventing a phone number or social account.

## Brand Commitments

- Business name: Choookies Dough.
- The supplied blue-and-white logo is the primary brand asset.
- Voice: cheerful, generous and reassuring, retaining the logo phrase "Don't worry, I gotchu!".

## Evidence on Hand

- Supplied logo at `assets/logo.png`.
- Supplied cookie photographs in `assets/`.
- Supplied package and price reference image in the original brief.
- No testimonials, sales claims, delivery information or contact details were supplied and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Let real cookie texture and size do the selling.
- Make the product choices and package prices easy to scan.
- Keep the path to ordering direct and friendly.
